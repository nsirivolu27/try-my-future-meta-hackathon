# Product standard

The ambition is to become the most-used AI campus tour guide in the United States. This is a product direction, not a current ranking or an adoption claim.

Do not feature or preselect particular schools. Discovery begins with the student’s choice from the national directory. Richer sourced content must not become a promotional ranking.

Earn repeat use by helping students understand a real day at a school: places to study, how to get involved, what a commute feels like, what to ask and what to compare later. Make the service useful to transfer students, community-college visitors and families as well as four-year university applicants.

Before expanding promotion, prove the core experience with students on actual campus visits:

- Answers concern the correct campus, cite relevant university evidence and admit unknowns.
- Voice input and output work on the hardware being advertised; measure end-of-speech to first spoken answer, failures and recovery on real connections.
- Students can complete a visit without onboarding help and keep useful observations.
- Visits remain useful when internet, model access or glasses are unavailable.
- Detailed guide coverage is clearly distinguished from institution-directory coverage.

Measure completed visits, repeat visits, helpful-answer feedback, verified-source coverage and voice success rate. For national adoption, track active campus visitors over a defined period and compare against credible market data before making any “number one” claim. No adoption analytics or user tracking has been added by this document.

# Try My Future

An AI campus-guide prototype for the **[Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon](https://events.cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon)**, October 26–November 4, 2026. Submission deadline: **November 4, 9 PM ET**.

The idea: **Try My Future @ [college]** — explore a campus around your prospective major and personal interests, with a guide you can talk to through your glasses.

## Progress · October 27

- Built: search across 5,877 institutions, visit activities, local notes and visit comparison. Detailed sourced guides currently cover three campuses.
- Implemented: Muse Spark Q&A and experimental Android / Meta glasses integration. **Live AI and physical glasses testing remain pending.**
- Checked locally: 29 tests, type checking, browser and Android builds, and Android lint passed.
